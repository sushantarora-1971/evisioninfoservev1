Built for the AI era

Designed for people. Structured for machines.

Every site we ship comes with the technical scaffolding search engines and AI models need to crawl, trust and cite your content — baked in from the first commit.

  • JSON-LD schema everywhere — Organization, Service, FAQPage, Article, Breadcrumb.
  • llms.txt + crawler rules — guide GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot & Google-Extended.
  • Answer-first content — a direct 40–60 word answer near the top of every page.
  • Core Web Vitals — fast LCP, INP & CLS for ranking and a great UX.
